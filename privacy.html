Privacy policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Random Frame desktop application handles data.

1. Data collection

Random Frame does not collect personal data or transmit personal data to its own servers.

The application does not:

  • create user accounts,
  • use analytics,
  • display advertising,
  • collect telemetry,
  • profile users.

2. Local application data

Random Frame stores a small amount of application state locally on your device for normal application functionality.

This may include:

  • the selected theme,
  • acknowledgement of the entry notice,
  • browsing history,
  • viewing statistics and the Prnt.sc identifiers already explored.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to Random Frame for analytics, advertising or profiling.

3. External services

Random Frame connects directly from your device to external image and content providers in order to retrieve content.

As part of a normal network connection, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, request details and device or network information. Random Frame does not receive or store this information.

Data processed by external providers is subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Saved files

Random Frame saves files only when you explicitly choose to do so and only to a location selected by you.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to:

contact@amokrzycki.ovh

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Random Frame's functionality or data processing practices change.

The application will include the current version of this policy.

Random Frame does not collect or transmit personal data to its own servers.
