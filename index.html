      Sync needs an upgrade

      Nearby Frames needs a newer Sync format. Update your other devices before upgrading. Older versions will stop syncing and keep their local data.

      The device list does not confirm which apps have been updated. Your local changes are waiting to sync.

      • History, saved frames and collections
      • Previously viewed and checked IDs
      • Activity & Stats
      • Preferences: theme and history page size

      Everything is encrypted before it leaves this device. Images saved to files aren’t backed up, so a Sync restores your lists, not the files.

      Start a new Sync

      Other devices need a version of Random Frame that supports Nearby Frames.

      Connect this device

      Create a pairing request and send it to your group’s administrator. They’ll send back an invitation to paste below. A legacy recovery key still works for an old Sync.

      Nothing is saved on this device yet, so it will take on the copy already in Sync.

      How should this device join?

      With Restore, if Sync was saved by an older app, data it could not save is kept from this device.

      Use the administration code on a new device to recover the same administrator identity. This replaces the former device’s access and restores the group’s data here. Local data will be replaced after recovery succeeds.

      Your recovery key

      Keep this key somewhere safe. Use it to bring your saved data to another device. Anyone with this key can connect to your Sync.

      Check the box below to finish.

      Linked devices

      Last synced is a record of past activity, not online presence.

        Add a device

        1. On the new device, open Sync → Connect this device → Generate pairing request.
        2. Paste its request below. Creating an invitation approves that device.
        3. Send the invitation back, then connect on the new device.

        To add another device, ask this group’s administrator to approve its pairing request.

        Keep your administration code safe. It lets you recover administration on another device.

        Move to managed Sync

        This device becomes the administrator of a new group. The old key cannot prove who created the old group. Your data and deletions are preserved; other devices must join again. Creating the new group does not close the old one. This is separate from a Sync format upgrade.

        The administrator cannot disconnect. To move administration, use your administration code on another device. To end Sync for everyone, dissolve this group.

        Remove device

        It will lose future read and write access. Previously downloaded data stays on that device. Other devices update their key automatically.

        Dissolve this group?

        This closes Sync for every device. Downloaded data and local files remain on each device. The group cannot be reopened.

        This device will stop syncing. The data on this device and your Sync data on the server stay as they are. To connect again, you’ll need a new invitation for managed Sync, or your legacy recovery key.
